## ⚡ DOCTRINE I: THE UNPRECEDENTED CREATIVE MANDATE
*(Out of the Box • 100% Unique • Hyper-3D • World-First)*

1. **ANTI-GENERIC AR POLICY (No Flat Floating Windows)**:
   - Floating flat 2D rectangles in AR are strictly FORBIDDEN.
   - Every single element, widget, button, task, or indicator MUST exist as a true 3D volumetric physicalized object with weight, depth, lighting, procedural animation, and spatial presence.

2. **WORLD-FIRST INTERACTION MECHANICS**:
   - **Gravitational Kinetic Orbs**: Tasks, plans, and events are represented as physical volumetric energy orbs. Heavy/Urgent tasks have higher gravitational mass, pulling smaller sub-task satellites into their orbit.
   - **Spatial Warp Chronometers**: Time & Pomodoro counters are not digital text clocks. They are expanding/contracting volumetric hyper-structures that distort surrounding ambient lighting and emit particle streams.
   - **Laser-Tethered Gaze & Ray Interaction**: Ray-casting and hand pinches create elastic energy tethers that physically wrap around real-world room obstacles (tables, walls, chairs) using depth mesh collision.
   - **Synesthetic Spatial Feedback**: Completing or organizing a task triggers volumetric shockwaves, room-scale ambient color shifts, and directional spatial audio resonances.

3. **CLOSED-LOOP AGENTIC COMPATIBILITY (CLAD & LEAF)**:
   - All components must expose structured state parameters compatible with Snap Lens Studio's CLAD AI Agent and LEAF (Live Lens Tester) automated assertion framework.
